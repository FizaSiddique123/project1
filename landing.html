How It Works

What is Clinical Triage AI?

Clinical Triage AI helps users organize information about their symptoms and understand the potential urgency of their situation through AI-assisted clinical triage.

The platform helps users understand what information may be relevant, how their symptoms can be organized, what level of attention may be appropriate, and when professional medical evaluation may be worth considering.

Important: Clinical Triage AI does not replace a doctor or provide definitive medical diagnosis.

Healthcare information can be overwhelming.

People often struggle to understand:

  • Which symptoms are important
  • Whether something needs attention
  • What information to prepare before speaking with a professional
  • How to organize their health information

Clinical Triage AI helps organize this information into a clearer experience.

AI That Supports — Not Replaces

Clinical Triage AI is designed to provide AI-assisted information and triage support.

It should not be treated as a diagnosis or a replacement for professional medical care.

For emergencies, users should seek appropriate emergency medical services rather than relying on the application.
